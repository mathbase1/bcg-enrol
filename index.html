      Enrolment process and FAQ reminders

      Core sequence

      1. Register.
      2. Speak to a curriculum tutor and confirm the right course.
      3. Complete Maths and English checks.
      4. Visit SEND/Student Support if required.
      5. Complete the enrolment link if it has not already been completed.
      6. Complete enrolment with MIS.

      Course and entry decisions

      • Confirm that the course supports the learner’s longer-term goals and that the published entry requirement is met.
      • Normally do not enrol a learner who does not meet the published entry requirement. Refer exceptional cases through the Curriculum Manager/FD/Exec and Fair Access Panel route where appropriate.
      • Help walk-in learners and learners changing course to find a suitable option; do not simply turn them away.
      • Do not assume a course is full unless an FD has explicitly confirmed that enrolment has stopped.

      Maths, English and group problems

      • Use the learner’s highest verified prior attainment and identify whether the allocation is Functional Skills or GCSE.
      • If the required group cell is blacked out, never place the learner in that group.
      • If the entry requirement is not met, refer back to the Main Vocational Enrolment Desk/vocational tutor.
      • If the entry requirement is met but no suitable group exists, contact Penny first, then Amy Joicey, then Hannah James.
      • Age 18 alone does not remove the Maths and English requirement on a study programme.

      SEND and the enrolment card

      • Any “Yes” in the three SEND questions means SEND/Student Support must be visited before enrolment is completed.
      • “No” to all three means place a red sticker on the enrolment card.
      • Once confirmed, apply the course label, write the main group, and write each Maths/English group code with FS or GCSE clearly identified. If labels run out, write the course code.

      Other quick guidance

      • Do not guess about funding; use the Enrolment Toolkit and refer uncertainty to MIS.
      • An apprenticeship applicant with an employer arranged goes to the Apprenticeship Team. Without an employer, discuss an appropriate college course.
      • StartUp induction is 2–4 September. Timetables may change, and a learner who misses an allocated session can attend another enrolment day.
