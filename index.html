      Enrolment process reminders

      Core sequence

      1. Register.
      2. Speak to a curriculum tutor and confirm the right route/course.
      3. Complete any required Maths and English checks.
      4. Complete any separate college support check that applies.
      5. Complete the enrolment link if not already done.
      6. Complete enrolment with MIS.

      16–19 route

      • Use the latest black-cell rules and the learner’s highest verified prior attainment.
      • No grade/U/Entry Level/grade 1 normally routes to Functional Skills; grade 2 to GCSE Plus; grade 3 to GCSE Resit; grade 4+ needs no compulsory GCSE group.
      • Functional Skills Level 1 is grade-3 equivalent for entry but routes to Plus.
      • English Literature grade 4+ means English Language is not compulsory, but the learner may request it voluntarily.
      • Inclusive Practice: Pathway to Progression, Accelerate and ESOL Cross College learners with confirmed Entry 3 or above may progress to Level 1. ESOL Community learners at E1/E2 must return to the Inclusive Practice table.
      • Construction: Functional Skills progression is only for the confirmed Multi Skills route; other Level 1 construction routes require GCSE evidence. Contact Kieran Sharps on Teams when unsure.
      • Accelerate Group D has a special one-subject GCSE option using G3. Do not allocate G3 to both subjects without Inclusive Practice review.
      • If entry is not met, stop and refer to the Main Vocational Enrolment Desk. If entry is met but a required cell is blacked out, contact Penny, then Amy Joicey, then Hannah James.

      Adult route

      • Use the attached full course code, ProSolution offering, permitted group list and qualification ID shown by the app.
      • Select the exact live group in ProSolution; do not use a group outside the supplied permitted list.
      • Do not treat a capped number as proof that a course is closed unless an FD explicitly confirms enrolment has stopped.
      • Do not guess funding. Open the Teacher Toolkit and refer uncertainty to MIS.

      Home-schooled route

      • AY means Aylesbury; FH course codes and WY college-level names mean High Wycombe.
      • Choose the exact teacher/time and write the whole course code shown after selection.
      • For Maths and English together, avoid overlapping sessions; the app flags direct timetable clashes.
      • Qualification IDs are deliberately not displayed.

      Final card and live checks

      • Apply the course label and write the correct group or full course code exactly as shown.
      • This streamlined app does not collect the separate support-triage answers; complete that college process outside the app where required.
      • Use the Teacher Toolkit button for live enrolment and funding guidance, and escalate uncertainty rather than guessing.
